# Tableau Dashboard Project (Data Science Salaries  Analysis)

## Project Overview
This project uses Tableau to explore data, identify trends, and communicate business insights through interactive visualizations.

## Objectives
- Analyze important business metrics.
- Identify trends and patterns.
- Compare performance across categories.
- Support data-driven decision-making.

## Tools Used
- Tableau Desktop
- Microsoft Excel / CSV (as applicable)
- Data Visualization and Data Analysis

## Key Insights
1. Employee Residence: The United States has the highest number of employee records, with approximately 332 records, followed by the United Kingdom (44) and India (30).
2. Company Size: Medium-sized companies represent the largest share of company records at 53.71%, followed by large companies at 32.62% and small companies at 13.67%.
3. Experience Level: Senior-level employees account for the largest share of records at 46.13%, followed by intermediate-level employees at 35.09%.
4. Employment Type: Full-time employment dominates the dataset, accounting for 96.87% of records, while part-time and freelance roles represent much smaller shares.
5. Salary Analysis: Average salaries vary across experience levels and employment types. The dashboard allows users to compare salary patterns and explore differences by company location and job title
